FAQ

Questions

Do I need to build a working automation?

No. A working prototype is great, but a well-reasoned exploration with screenshots and honest feedback can win too, especially in the Sharpest Feedback category.

Can I submit as a team?

Entries are individual. If you collaborated, submit under one name and credit your teammates in the entry. The voucher goes to the submitter.

Can I cover more than one product?

Yes. Combining capabilities, such as Cartographer feeding Maestro, is encouraged.

Is this run by UiPath?

This is a community-run challenge. UiPath supports it by providing the prize vouchers.

Can I edit my entry after submitting?

Submit again before the deadline and mention in the form that it replaces your earlier entry. The latest one counts.

Who do I contact with questions?

Message the organizer on LinkedIn or comment on the challenge post.
